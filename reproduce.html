Reproducing the Publication

All figures and tables of the publication and its Supporting Information that are computed from data are generated by one script, run from any folder:

python create_manuscript_plots_tables.py            # all figures and tables
python create_manuscript_plots_tables.py --list     # list the steps
python create_manuscript_plots_tables.py --only fig3 tabS4
python create_manuscript_plots_tables.py --show     # also display the figures

Figures are written to figures/ as SVG files and tables to tables/ as CSV files; both folders are not tracked by git. Figures 4 and 5 were arranged in Inkscape for the publication.

Data

The measurement and simulation data used by most steps are part of this repository (results/). Three groups of files are too large for git and are part of the Zenodo dataset; steps whose data are missing are skipped with a message:

  • Figure 5: results/geometric/<LED>/optimization_results_MonteCarlo_1-16_leds_5-15_cm_5000000_samples.npz or the corresponding simulation_results_...npz for both LED types.

  • Table S5 and the convergence study (Supporting Information S11 and S12, Figures S18 and S19): the samples and metrics of the four seeds in results/geometric/monte_carlo_convergence/ (mc_seed<seed>_10000000_configs.npz and mc_seed<seed>_10000000_metrics_<UV|GRN>.npz, about 2.1 GB). --recompute draws and evaluates the missing ones with analysis.mc_sampling (about two hours on two cores for all four seeds, about 2 GB of memory per run); delete existing files to redo them.

  • The test of the published sample (see below): the published 5 million-sample files results/sampled_configs/MonteCarlo_1-16_leds_5-15_cm_5000000_samples.npz and results/geometric/<LED>/simulation_results_MonteCarlo_1-16_leds_5-15_cm_5000000_samples.npz.

Numbers in the Text

The tables written to tables/ contain the numbers quoted in the text, for example:

  • Model Validation: the deviation of the homogeneity between geometric model and radiometry (metrics_all_methods.csv, columns model_H and rad_H); the RMSE of the residual fields (difference_summary.csv); the systematic effects of the radiometry, 6 %-19 % and 4.2 %-5.1 % in mean irradiance and -0.006 to +0.031 in homogeneity (table_S4_radiometry_corrections.csv); the effect of the ray-tracing noise on the homogeneity (ray_tracing_noise.csv).

  • Effect of the reflectors: gains of 9 %-38 % (reflector_effects.csv) and the spread between UV1-UV3 and GRN1-GRN3 with and without reflectors (reflector_spread.csv).

  • Section S11: the highest local irradiance of the 8-LED study and of UV1-UV3 / GRN1-GRN3 (irradiance_bound_8_led_maxima.csv, irradiance_bound_selected_maxima.csv).

  • Section S12: the objective score of the best configuration per seed and sample size (mc_best_configurations.csv) and the agreement of the fronts (mc_front_deviation.csv).

Tests

Two checks back statements of the Supporting Information:

  • tests/test_fast_evaluation.py: the fast evaluation of analysis.fast_evaluation, used for the Monte-Carlo studies, reproduces the stored results of the geometric model.

  • tests/test_seed42_published.py: the first 5 million configurations of seed 42 are the published sample, row by row, and their metrics equal the stored ones (needs the Zenodo files; skipped otherwise).

pip install pytest
python -m pytest tests
